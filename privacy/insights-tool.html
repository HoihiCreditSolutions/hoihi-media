Hoihi Insights: Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Hoihi Insights is an internal reporting tool run by Hoihi Credit Solutions in Waiʻanae, Hawaiʻi. It reads the performance numbers for Hoihi's own social media accounts and saves them to a private spreadsheet, so we can see how our posts are doing. This policy covers only this tool. It does not cover our client services or our website.

Whose data it reads

Only accounts that Hoihi Credit Solutions owns and has signed in to the tool itself: our Facebook Page, Instagram account, YouTube channel and TikTok account. The tool is not offered to the public, and no one outside our business can connect an account to it.

What it reads

  • Basic account details: account name and follower count.
  • Our own posts and videos: title or caption, publish date and link.
  • Performance totals for those posts: views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves and average watch time.

These are totals only. The tool does not read the names, profiles or messages of people who view, like or comment on our posts, and it does not read anyone's private messages.

How it is used

To see which of our posts help people and to plan future posts. The tool only reads. It cannot post, edit, delete, send messages or run ads.

Where it is stored

In a private Google Sheet in Hoihi's Google Workspace account, viewable only by Hoihi staff. The keys that let the tool read our accounts are stored encrypted and are never shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long it is kept

We keep daily totals for as long as they are useful for comparing our posts over time, and we delete them when they are not. You can ask us to delete anything the tool has saved by emailing us.

Turning it off

We can disconnect the tool from any of our accounts at any time, in that platform's settings, and it then stops reading that account.

Questions about this policy: info@hoihicreditsolutions.com.
